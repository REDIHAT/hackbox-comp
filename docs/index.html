How to Flash Your Device

  • 1
    Connect Device: Connect your ESP32 board to your computer using a quality USB data cable (ensure the cable supports data, not just charging).
  • 2
    Select Port: Click "CONNECT & FLASH HACKBOX" above and choose your board's serial port (e.g. CP2102, CH340, or USB JTAG/serial) from the popup.
  • 3
    Bootloader Mode: If connection fails to establish, hold down the BOOT button on your ESP32 while clicking connect, then release it once flashing starts.
  • 4
    Automatic Setup: The flasher will automatically erase, write the merged firmware, and verify the installation. When finished, unplug and restart your device!
